1. OUR SERVICES

Story Reader is a web-based application designed for children and their parents. The app allows children to describe a story idea using voice or text input, which is used to generate a custom, age-appropriate short story using AI (Anthropic's Claude). Children then read the story aloud, and the app uses speech recognition to track their reading progress and highlight words in real time.

Parents create and manage the account, set reading level preferences, and can view their child's reading history, streaks, and progress through a parent dashboard protected by a PIN. The app includes an optional social feature that allows children to connect with approved friends, share stories they've read, and react to friends' shared content — all of which requires parental setup and approval.
