PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated September 09, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Blibbo LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at http://www.storyreader.app or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use storyreader.app. Story Reader is a web-based application designed for children and their parents. The app allows children to describe a story idea using voice or text input, which is used to generate a custom, age-appropriate short story using AI (Anthropic's Claude). Children then read the story aloud, and the app uses speech recognition to track their reading progress and highlight words in real time. Parents create and manage the account, set reading level preferences, and can view their child's reading history, streaks, and progress through a parent dashboard protected by a PIN. The app includes an optional social feature that allows children to connect with approved friends, share stories they've read, and react to friends' shared content — all of which requires parental setup and approval.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at privacy@storyreader.app.

12. AI-GENERATED CONTENT

Story Reader uses artificial intelligence, provided by Anthropic (the developer of Claude), to generate personalized stories based on story ideas submitted by users. While we strive to ensure generated content is age-appropriate, AI-generated stories may occasionally contain unexpected or imperfect content. We encourage parents and guardians to review stories alongside their child and to contact us if any content is found to be inappropriate.

13. VOICE AND MICROPHONE DATA

Story Reader uses your device's microphone and speech recognition technology to track a child's reading progress in real time. Voice input is processed live to match spoken words against story text and is not recorded, stored, or retained as an audio file. No audio recordings are saved on our servers or by any third party.

14. PARENTAL CONSENT AND ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Story Reader is designed for use by children under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. A parent or legal guardian must create and manage the account, and is responsible for reviewing account settings, setting reading preferences, and approving any social features, including friend requests, on behalf of the child. Story Reader does not knowingly allow children to create or manage their own accounts independently.

15. THIRD-PARTY AI PROCESSING

Story ideas submitted by users, whether typed or spoken, are transmitted to Anthropic's API for the purpose of generating story content. This data is used solely to generate the requested story and is subject to Anthropic's own data handling practices. Story Reader does not use submitted story ideas to train any AI models.
